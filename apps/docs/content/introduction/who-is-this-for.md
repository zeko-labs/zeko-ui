As Zeko has a whide target audience, this documentation is divided into sections tailored for different roles. Whether you're a user, developer, network operator, or just curious about zero-knowledge and blockchain concepts, you'll find relevant information here.:

- [Beginners: Understand the fundamentals of zero-knowledge and blockchain.](../background/getting-started.md)
- [Users: Learn how to use Zeko wallets, bridges, and dApps.](../users/getting-started.md)
- [Developers: Build, deploy, and interact with zkApps on Zeko.](../developers/getting-started.md)
- [Network Operators: Run nodes and participate in the Zeko network.](../operators/getting-started.md)

  _Navigate to the section that matches your role for a tailored experience!_
